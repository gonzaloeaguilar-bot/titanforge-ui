Frequently Asked Questions

What does Train With Intention mean?

It means the plan has a reason: training, nutrition, recovery, and accountability are adjusted from feedback instead of random effort or random intensity.

Does the method depend on supplements?

No. Titan Forge treats supplements as support tools after training, food, sleep, hydration, recovery, and consistency are handled.

What feedback changes the plan?

Training performance, adherence, hunger, soreness, sleep, schedule, body-weight trends, technique confidence, and recovery all matter more than a single perfect-looking spreadsheet.

Why does the method start with assessment?

Assessment prevents the plan from pretending every client has the same history, skill level, constraints, or tolerance for change.

How often should a plan change?

A plan should change when the feedback justifies it. Random changes create noise; clear trends and honest reporting create useful coaching decisions.
