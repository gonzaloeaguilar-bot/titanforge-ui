Frequently Asked Questions

What does the trust-boundary cluster answer?

It answers refund, cancellation, pause, guarantee, testimonial, agreement, communication, and post-application expectation questions without inventing unapproved terms.

Why add testimonial-reading guidance?

Reviews and testimonials can support trust, but they should not be treated as guaranteed, typical, or identical future outcomes.

What should coaching agreements clarify?

They should clarify scope, communication, privacy, payment, cancellation or pause rules, referral limits, and what outcomes are not guaranteed.
