Frequently Asked Questions

Is body recomposition different from weight loss?

Yes. Weight loss focuses on scale direction. Recomposition also values strength, lean-mass retention, measurements, photos when appropriate, and training quality.

What should I track for recomposition?

Track body-weight trend, waist or measurements, strength performance, nutrition adherence, protein, sleep, recovery, and photos when appropriate.

Should I diet harder if the scale is flat?

Not automatically. First check adherence, recovery, soreness, sodium, digestion, and other progress signals before changing calories.

When is maintenance useful?

Maintenance can be useful after a fat-loss phase when hunger, performance, recovery, or adherence need time to stabilize before the next push.
