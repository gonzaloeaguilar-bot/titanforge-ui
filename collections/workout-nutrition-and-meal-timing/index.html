Frequently Asked Questions

What should I read first for workout nutrition?

Start with pre-workout meals if sessions feel flat, post-workout nutrition if recovery structure is missing, or meal timing for busy lifters if schedule is the problem.

Does timing matter more than daily intake?

No. Timing can make it easier to stick to, but total daily intake, protein consistency, hydration, and adherence still set the bigger direction.

Are carbs required before every workout?

No. They are most useful when they improve hard-session quality, repeated performance, or adherence without causing digestion or calorie-budget problems.

Can fasted training work?

It can work when performance, safety, and adherence stay intact. It should not be used as a magic fat-loss shortcut.

When should coaching help with meal timing?

When eating windows, work schedule, training time, appetite, and recovery keep colliding and the person cannot tell which lever to adjust.
