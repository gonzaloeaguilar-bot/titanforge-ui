Frequently Asked Questions

What does the credentials and vetting cluster answer?

It answers how to check fitness coach credentials, what certifications mean, what red flags to watch for, how to verify online claims, and which privacy or communication boundaries should be clear.

What should buyers verify before applying?

Buyers should verify credentials, scope, communication expectations, proof context, guarantee language, privacy boundaries, and how the coach handles referral needs.

How do reviews, credentials, and claims differ?

Reviews support business trust, credentials support training education, and claims need visible evidence plus limitations. None of them should become a guaranteed outcome.
