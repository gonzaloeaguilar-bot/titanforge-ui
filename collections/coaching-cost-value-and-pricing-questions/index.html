What should online fitness coaching cost actually buy?

Online coaching is worth paying for when the fee buys individualized decisions: assessment, programming, form or follow-through feedback, useful check-ins, schedule-aware adjustments, and accountability between workouts. Price alone cannot establish value; compare what changes when your real what actually happened comes back.

Important limitation: A monthly number is not a result guarantee, and Titan Forge does not treat a generic market price as proof that a service fits every person.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Titan Forge coaching cost?

Titan Forge lists Premium Coaching at $750/mo, Standard Coaching at $400/mo, Nutrition Only at $200/mo, and App + Community at $49.99/mo.

Why avoid generic market prices?

Generic prices ignore coaching format, support level, nutrition scope, local versus online fit, and written agreement terms, so the approved package ladder is the safer source.

What affects coaching value?

Value depends on assessment, feedback, support level, nutrition scope, accountability, local versus online format, and how often the plan needs human adjustment.

How should buyers compare coaching to apps?

Apps and templates can work for self-directed users, while coaching is more valuable when the missing piece is judgment, accountability, feedback, or consistency.
