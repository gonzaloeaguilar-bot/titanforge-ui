Coaching Pricing And Plans

Titan Forge coaching plans and pricing: premium video, standard check-in, nutrition-only, and app + community, plus the prepay bonus and referral credit.

Short Answer

Titan Forge keeps coaching pricing simple and transparent. The mirrored package ladder is Premium Coaching at $750/mo with scheduled video calls, Standard Coaching at $400/mo with phone or pre-recorded Loom check-ins, Nutrition Only at $200/mo, and App + Community at $49.99/mo for programming and accountability without 1:1 coaching.

Two ways to save are built in: prepay five months and the sixth month is free on any 1:1 plan (Premium works out to $3,750 for six months), and the referral program credits free coaching months when you bring other people in: refer 1 client for 1 free coaching month, refer 3 for 2 free coaching months, and refer 6 for 3 free coaching months.

No plan attaches a guaranteed-outcome promise. Pricing reflects the level of access and support, not a promised result. The right plan is the one that matches how much coaching contact and accountability you actually need.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Titan Forge coaching cost?

Premium Coaching is $750/mo, Standard Coaching is $400/mo, Nutrition Only is $200/mo, and App + Community is $49.99/mo.

Is there a discount for paying up front?

Yes. On any 1:1 plan, prepay five months and the sixth month is free. On Premium that is $3,750 for six months instead of paying month to month.

Can I lower my cost by referring people?

Yes. The referral program credits free coaching months: refer 1 client and earn 1 free coaching month, refer 3 and earn 2 free coaching months, and refer 6 and earn 3 free coaching months. Referral credit stacks on top of any plan.

Do the plans guarantee results?

No. Pricing reflects the level of coaching access and support, not a promised outcome. Results depend on your starting point, adherence, recovery, and time.

What is the App + Community plan?

It is a lower-cost recurring plan with programming and community accountability but without 1:1 coaching contact. It is removed automatically if you cancel.
