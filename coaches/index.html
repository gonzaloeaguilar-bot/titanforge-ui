Meet Steve "Doc" Ginevan, PhD Coaching

Titan Forge Fitness is founded and led by Steve "Doc" Ginevan, PhD — analytical, evidence-driven coaching for training, nutrition, and accountability.

Short Answer

Titan Forge Fitness is founded and led by Steve "Doc" Ginevan, PhD. He runs the analytical, evidence-driven lane: a methodical, data-first approach for clients who want structure, measurement, and a clear read on training and nutrition.

The coaching covers assessment, a plan built around your actual constraints, weekly check-ins, and honest feedback. Training volume, exercise selection, and nutrition anchors get adjusted from measured trends rather than from how a single day felt.

If you are not sure whether coaching is the right next step, that is normal. Apply and Steve reviews your goal, training history, and schedule before any call, then maps the starting point that fits — coaching, a membership, or free resources.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who coaches at Titan Forge?

Steve "Doc" Ginevan, PhD is the founder and head coach. He runs an analytical, evidence-driven, data-first lane covering training, nutrition, and accountability.

How do I know if Titan Forge coaching is right for me?

It depends on your goals, schedule, and how you like to be coached. Titan Forge suits people who want structure, measurement, and a science-first read on training and nutrition. Apply and Steve reviews the fit before any call.

What does the analytical coaching approach look like in practice?

Assessment first, then a plan built around your constraints, then weekly check-ins where training volume, exercise selection, and nutrition anchors get adjusted from what the data actually shows.

Can the plan change once coaching starts?

Yes. The plan is expected to change. Check-in data decides whether to hold, progress, deload, substitute exercises, or simplify the week.

Do all Titan Forge coaches follow the same standards?

Yes. Every coaching lane under Titan Forge holds the same coaching standards and claim hygiene — no guaranteed-outcome promises and no fabricated proof.
