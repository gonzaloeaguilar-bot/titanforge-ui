How Far Is The Gym From Parker?

About 7.4 miles, or roughly 14 minutes with no traffic, from Parker Town Hall at 20120 E Mainstreet. Most of the route runs west along East Lincoln Avenue. The trip home is routed by E-470 and comes to about 8.3 miles and 13 minutes. Both figures are routing estimates read on October 13, 2026: the addresses were matched with the US Census geocoder and the route calculated with the OSRM engine on OpenStreetMap roads.

Rush hour on Lincoln Avenue adds time, so check the trip in your own map app at the hour you would actually train. If the drive makes three gym visits a week unrealistic, that is a good reason to look at online coaching, or at a mix of both, before you commit to sessions you will struggle to reach.

When Is The Gym Open?

The hours Titan Forge lists for the Englewood gym are Monday to Friday 5:00 am to 9:00 pm, Saturday 7:00 am to 4:00 pm, and Sunday 8:00 am to 3:00 pm. Those are the doors, not a promise of a particular session slot: training times are agreed with your coach during the application conversation, and the Google Business Profile linked further down this page is the place to confirm the hours before your first visit.

For someone driving in from Parker, the early weekday opening matters most. A 6:00 am session at the gym means leaving Parker shortly after 5:40 am on a clear road, early enough to train before a normal workday. Weekend hours are shorter, so plan Saturday and Sunday sessions earlier in the day.

What Does It Cost, And Should I Train In Person Or Online?

In-person sessions have no published price: the pricing page lists no per-session rate, so ask about session pricing when you apply. The published monthly plans are online coaching, and none of them includes gym sessions. Premium Coaching is $750/mo with scheduled video calls, Standard Coaching is $400/mo with phone or pre-recorded Loom check-ins, Nutrition Only is $200/mo, and App + Community is $49.99/mo. Prepay five months on any 1:1 plan and the sixth month is free.

Choose in person if hands-on technique feedback and a fixed session time keep you consistent. Choose online if the drive or your schedule makes regular visits unrealistic. You can also combine the two: in-person sessions for the lifts they want a coach to watch, with online programming and check-ins for the rest of the week. To start, apply for coaching. What should you look for in personal training near Englewood, CO?

Start with the constraint you need the trainer to solve: live technique feedback, gym confidence, a repeatable strength plan, nutrition context, or accountability between sessions. Ask how assessment, progression, communication, missed weeks, and plan adjustments work before deciding whether local sessions or online coaching fit better. Bring your training history, available equipment, weekly schedule, and the specific obstacle that keeps interrupting progress so the first conversation can start with a practical fit decision.
