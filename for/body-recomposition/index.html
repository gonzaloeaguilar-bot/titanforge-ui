Short Answer

Body Recomposition Coaching at Titan Forge Fitness is a structured, evidence-led process designed to build muscle and reduce fat simultaneously. Founded and led by Steve "Doc" Ginevan, PhD, Titan Forge structures progressive resistance training, individualized protein targets, and recovery habits around real-world constraints—eliminating the destructive cycle of extreme crash dieting followed by aggressive bulk phases.

Because simultaneous muscle gain and fat loss often keep total scale weight relatively steady, progress is guided by multi-point measurement trends (lifting performance logs, waist measurements, recovery markers, and structured check-ins) rather than reactionary day-to-day scale fluctuations.

What Is Included In Coaching To Lose Fat And Build Muscle At The Same Time?

Titan Forge body recomposition coaching provides the complete training, nutrition, and accountability structure needed to reshape body composition sustainably:

  • Customized Progressive Strength Programming: Individualized workout splits (typically 3 to 4 days weekly) built around multi-joint compound movements, monitored progressive overload, and repeatable movement mechanics.
  • Evidence-Based Nutritional Architecture: Target protein baselines (1.6–2.2g/kg or 0.8–1.0g per pound of body weight) coupled with tailored calorie ranges—avoiding extreme deficits that compromise muscle retention.
  • Multi-Signal Metric Tracking System: Regular logging of gym performance metrics, waist circumference trends, sleep, and fatigue markers to evaluate body composition changes beyond scale weight.
  • Weekly Check-In Reviews & Feedback: Direct feedback reviewing adherence, how training actually went, recovery, and lifestyle stressors, with adjustments made to training volume or nutrition targets.
  • Video Movement & Technique Feedback: Video analysis of key lifts to ensure safe follow-through, proper range of motion, and effective mechanical tension under load.
  • Lifestyle, Travel & Restaurant Strategies: Practical food and routine defaults to navigate work travel, client dinners, and busy weekends without falling off track.

How Do You Lose Fat And Build Muscle At The Same Time?

We approach body recomposition as a clear coaching process structured across five distinct stages:

  1. Intake & Comprehensive Assessment: We evaluate your lifting background, injury history, typical schedule, metabolic baseline, and daily nutritional patterns to understand your starting point.
  2. Baseline Target Calibration: We set achievable weekly training frequencies and evidence-supported daily protein and calorie targets suited for simultaneous muscle preservation and fat loss.
  3. Follow-through & Movement Mastery: You follow structured training sessions, log loads and repetitions, track nutritional compliance, and submit technique videos for review.
  4. Multi-Point Trend Analysis: Rather than reacting to daily scale fluctuations, we analyze 2 to 3 weeks of performance trends, waist measurements, and recovery data to measure true tissue changes.
  5. Iterative Calibration & Progression: We calibrate training loads, adjust volume, or refine macro targets when verified trend data indicates progress has plateaued.

What Does A Body Recomposition Plan Look Like Week To Week?

Sustainable changes in body composition require coordinating training stimulus with nutritional support and recovery management:

  • Progressive Tension & Targeted Volume: Resistance training is the non-negotiable stimulus that signals muscle retention and growth. Programs prioritize compound movements, repeatable technique, and progressive overload across structured 3–4 day weekly splits.
  • Protein Anchoring & Caloric Precision: Daily nutrition is built around evidence-based protein baselines (1.6–2.2g/kg or roughly 0.8–1.0g per pound of target body weight) paired with a modest, sustainable energy deficit or maintenance intake that fuels high-effort training without sacrificing lean tissue.
  • Multi-Signal Progress Tracking: Chasing the bathroom scale alone often leads to premature diet adjustments. We track waist circumference trends, gym load progression, sleep quality, and adherence data to evaluate true body composition changes.
  • Trend-Based Feedback & Weekly Adjustments: Coaching changes are made from 2 to 3 weeks of verified trend lines, allowing training and nutrition to adapt to schedule disruptions, work travel, and fatigue without restarting the plan.

Who Is Body Recomposition Coaching Right For?

Recomposition is particularly effective for trainees in specific training situations:

  • Beginners & Returning Lifters: Individuals new to structured lifting or returning after an extended layoff, where neuromuscular adaptations and muscle growth occur rapidly alongside fat loss. (Coaching for Beginners · Returning After Layoff)
  • Lifters Over 40: Adults aiming to improve metabolic health, build lean mass, and reduce visceral fat while managing joint recovery and daily energy. (Coaching for Men Over 40)
  • Busy Professionals: Trainees needing time-efficient, high-impact strength routines and simple nutrition anchors that fit demanding work and travel schedules. (Coaching for Busy Professionals)
  • Trainees Stuck in Dieting Loops: Lifters who have cycled through multiple restrictive cuts without seeing muscle definition and need a foundation-first resistance training approach. (Online Fitness Coaching)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is body recomposition coaching?

It is coaching for improving body composition by coordinating training, nutrition, recovery, and expectations rather than only chasing the scale.

Is recomposition the same as fat loss?

No. Fat loss focuses mainly on reducing body weight or body fat. Recomposition may include strength, muscle retention, muscle gain, waist changes, and performance signals.

Who is a good fit for recomposition?

People who want to look, feel, and perform better, especially when extreme dieting would hurt training consistency or recovery.

How long does recomposition take?

It is usually slower than a hard cut because the goal is better tissue, training, and habits, not only faster scale movement. No timeline should be guaranteed.

What gets tracked besides weight?

Strength performance, measurements, photos when appropriate, nutrition adherence, recovery, sleep, steps, and how consistently the plan is followed.

What is included in body recomposition coaching at Titan Forge?

Coaching includes an individualized strength program with progressive overload, evidence-based protein and calorie targets, multi-point progress tracking (waist measurements, gym performance, recovery), and weekly coach check-ins that adjust the plan based on verified trends.

Can you build muscle in a calorie deficit?

Yes, particularly for beginners, lifters returning from a layoff, or individuals with higher body fat, provided the deficit is moderate (10–15%), protein intake is anchored around 1.6–2.2g/kg, and resistance training applies progressive mechanical tension.

How does nutrition adjust during recomposition coaching?

Nutrition starts at maintenance or a conservative deficit based on initial assessment. Adjustments happen every 2 to 3 weeks based on trend data—adjusting calories, macros, or meal timing if recovery, strength, or waist trends stall.
